[b]Save Options saves your colony when something bad happens, saves when you quit, and loads your latest save when the game starts.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Save on negative events:[/b] Major threats and negative events save your colony to "Bad Event".
[*][b]Save and quit:[/b] The quit buttons save under your colony's name first. Hold Alt to quit without saving.
[*][b]Start save:[/b] Each new colony can be saved as "Start" once it has landed.
[*][b]Auto load:[/b] Your latest save loads when the game starts. Press any key during the countdown to stay at the menu.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Replaces Save on Negative Event, Save and Quit, and Auto Load Last Save, so disable them when using this mod.
Letters from Letter Permanent Injury don't trigger a negative-event save.
Safe to add to an existing save.
